## 4. How it works (3 steps)

**Section heading:** From "where am I?" to steady progress in three steps.

**Step 1 — Take the diagnostic**
A quick assessment places your level across vocabulary, grammar, and listening. Around five minutes.

**Step 2 — Practice your gaps**
The AI serves exercises targeted at your weak spots and spaces out review so it sticks. Difficulty shifts with you.

**Step 3 — Watch your level move**
Your progress is tracked per skill in CEFR bands, so improvement is visible — and the plan updates as you go.

**CTA under section:** Start with the diagnostic
