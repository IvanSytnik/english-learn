Last summer my family went to the seaside. We stayed in a small hotel near the beach.
Every morning my children played in the sand and I read books. The weather was hot and
sunny, but one day it rained, so we visited a museum in the town. In the evening we
usually ate fish in a cheap restaurant next to the bus stop. I bought some presents for
my friends and took a lot of photos. It was the best holiday we have had for years.
